# Creative Wizard Suite Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Creative Wizard Suite is a Figma plugin published by Eyal Levanon. For privacy questions, contact eyalevanon@gmail.com.

## What the plugin handles

When you use a tool, the plugin reads the Figma document content needed for that action. This may include selected layers, text, images, component information, names, positions, and other design properties. If you choose to import a PDF, SVG, or PSD, the plugin reads that file to create Figma content. If you choose to export or package work, it creates files for you to download. The plugin does not require an account separate from Figma.

## Where processing happens

The plugin processes design content inside the Figma plugin environment and your local plugin window. Its published manifest permits no network access, and the plugin has no developer-operated server, analytics service, or advertising tracker. It does not send design content to Eyal Levanon. Preview links are generated from the current Figma file; opening or sharing one is a user action and is subject to Figma's access controls and privacy practices.

## What is stored

The plugin uses Figma's per-user plugin storage for settings such as dashboard layout, Components options, Preflight rules, and ignored Preflight issues. Ignored-issue records can include a frame name, rule name, and issue signature. The plugin does not store imported files or exported artwork on a developer-operated server. Generated Figma objects remain in your Figma file, and downloaded exports remain wherever you save them. You can change or reset supported settings in the plugin; for other stored plugin data, use Figma's controls or contact support for guidance.

## Support and purchases

If you email support, Eyal Levanon receives your email address and whatever information you choose to include, and uses it to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending confidential client artwork unless it is necessary and you are authorized to share it. Figma and its payment providers handle Community purchases, subscriptions, and payout processing under their own policies; the plugin does not collect payment card information.

## Sharing, retention, and your choices

Eyal Levanon does not sell plugin usage data or design content. The plugin does not transmit your design content to a developer-operated service. Figma stores your files and plugin settings according to its services and controls. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to provide support, maintain a record of the request, and meet applicable obligations. You can avoid importing a file, choose which Figma content to process, delete generated objects or downloaded exports, and contact support about support correspondence. Preview links should be shared only with people you intend to give access to the underlying Figma file.

This policy describes Creative Wizard Suite's GIF and static export release. If a later version adds a network service or materially changes data handling, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
